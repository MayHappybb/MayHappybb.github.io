- **Spot Micro quadruped** (March 2021, Prof. Honghai Liu's lab). A low-cost servo quadruped rebuilt from nothing, alone: printed parts, servo calibration, ROS, SLAM mapping and gait switching.
- **Cable-driven parallel robot** (January 2021, Dr. Han Yuan's lab). Taught myself the kinematics: anchor coordinates, cable lengths and their Jacobian, then path interpolation and inverse kinematics in Python.
- **UR5e cup stacking** (September 2021, robotics course design). ROS and MoveIt Cartesian planning pick cups from a fixed point and stack them three high.
- **Smart logistics car** (2019–2020, team leader). A mecanum-wheel car with a 3D-printed 4-DoF arm, QR-code tasks, line tracking and PID motor control.
- **sEMG / FES acquisition board** (2020, Prof. Honghai Liu's lab). Two stacked Altium boards: an ADS1299 front end, an STM32 and Bluetooth to a PC, for reading muscle signals and driving electrical stimulation.
- **CUMCM 2020**, the national mathematical modelling contest: a reflow-oven temperature curve.
