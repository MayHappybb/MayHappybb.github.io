- **textfmt**: text copied from a PDF or a terminal comes out broken into lines. `pbpaste | textfmt | pbcopy` joins sentences again, keeps list items and fixes hyphen breaks.
- **Dither art**: a portrait for my CV made of dots and symbols, drawn in TikZ. The research behind it became the [dithering studio](/tools/dither/).
- **ML algorithm field guide**: a one-page HTML guide I made while reading Burkov's *Hundred-Page Machine Learning Book*.
- **Agent skills**: a PARA organiser for an Obsidian vault (it reorganised my 102 notes), a birthday-gift recommender that asks the right questions, an explainer-video skill, and the clock-face skills.
