A team of five built a wardrobe that stores and fetches boxes by itself: rotating vertical storage, a worm-gear lift to the upper level, folding hangers that share one movable motor, and a small app where you pick an item by its photo (the wardrobe takes one when you store it).

My parts: the H-bot gripper (two fixed steppers move the gripper in a plane, so the motors don't ride along), the foldable two-section conveyor that saves floor space, and a Python GUI that tuned each mechanism remotely over Wi-Fi. Those parameters went into the final program. National first prize, fischertechnik group, 10 October 2020.
