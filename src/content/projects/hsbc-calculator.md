New HSBC Hong Kong One accounts without a local ID must keep an average balance of HKD 10,000 over the first three full months, or pay a monthly fee. Working out "how much do I still need to deposit, and when" by hand is annoying.

Enter the opening date, the remittances and any withdrawals; it counts balance-days and tells you the extra deposit needed. Chinese and English, nothing leaves the browser, and the result can be saved as an image. When I shared it on Xiaohongshu, the post got about 6,000 views and over a hundred comments.
