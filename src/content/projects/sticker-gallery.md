I really enjoy how lively Telegram's animated stickers are, and wanted them elsewhere. The app renders packs server-side (rlottie and ffmpeg), has a frame scrubber, and exports GIF, MP4 and WebM at any size and frame rate, PNG or SVG frames, a whole pack as a ZIP, and a "WeChat preset": a 30 fps GIF at the highest resolution that stays under 4 MB.
