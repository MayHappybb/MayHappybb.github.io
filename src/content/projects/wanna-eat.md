In a lab of twenty-odd students, asking "lunch?" in the group chat meets awkward silence, and walking over to check who is in the office wastes time. **where2eat** (2024) was a one-evening Flask app for voting on the canteen. **wanna-eat** (2026) redid it properly with Next.js and live updates: everyone sets a hunger status and where they are (with privacy controls), ranks restaurants, and chats; a summary shows who is in and where to go.
