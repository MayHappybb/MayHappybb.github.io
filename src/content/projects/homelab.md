It started with rooting TV boxes in 2019. Now: an Ubuntu NAS that serves media (Jellyfin), files (Samba), the printer (CUPS) and backups from five machines; a small cloud VM that hosts my agents' MCP servers; a Radxa single-board computer; an Orange Pi for Home Assistant (unfinished); and a cheap TV box reflashed to CoreELEC as a media and download box. Reverse SSH tunnels and Tailscale connect them, so I can work on any of them from anywhere, even from a phone.
