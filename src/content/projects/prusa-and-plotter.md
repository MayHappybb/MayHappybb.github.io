In my day-to-day life I keep having small ideas and get curious about how things work. Out of curiosity about 3D printers, I assembled one myself during the 2020 home semester: a Prusa-style i3 on aluminium extrusion, running Marlin, sliced with PrusaSlicer.

The pen plotter started when I was bored at home: two stepper mechanisms from old CD drives, an Arduino with GRBL, and a servo to lift the pen. It became a third-year student project, an automatic writing and laser-engraving robot, which passed its final review. The later version in the photos uses printed parts and a larger XY frame.

The same year: an ESP8266 switch for our dorm light (the switch was hidden behind a bed, so my roommates could now flip it from their phones), HomeKit sensors over MQTT, an IR remote for the air conditioner, a Nano alarm clock, an INA219 power meter, a Bluetooth speaker in a printed case, and a macro-keypad PCB.
