- **VFD clock** (June 2023). Vacuum-fluorescent displays glow a lovely blue-green, but the filament wants AC. I simulated the filament drive in Multisim, drew the schematic, and wrote my own Arduino driver for the ESP8266 with animations and over-the-air updates.
- **Desktop PC rack** (March–April 2023). 1515 aluminium extrusion with acrylic panels and a hinged door, laser-cut, modelled together with the desk and the laptops as a little "desk digital twin" so everything fitted before cutting.
- Also that year: a laptop cooler, a Raspberry Pi Pico, an arithmetic-exercise generator typeset in LaTeX, and a production calculator for Dyson Sphere Program.
