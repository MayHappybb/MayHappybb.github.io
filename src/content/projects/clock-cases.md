A clock face needs a body. I like retro industrial design, so the concepts went that way: a mini CRT with a bezel, a cover, legs and a knob, and a Braun-style block. The mini CRT is a parametric OpenSCAD model (367 lines) that exports six STLs, with assembly fixes worked out from the drawing: the bezel became a chassis, pegs sit in slightly larger sockets, edges have print chamfers. A separate desk-clock case was measured straight from the LCD module's datasheet drawing.
