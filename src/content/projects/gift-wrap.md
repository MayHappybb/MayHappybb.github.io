For a friend's birthday I wanted wrapping paper made for them, and all the wrappers in the shops were uninteresting. So, one evening: a small editor where you place images, text, emoji and Telegram stickers on a tile, choose one of six tiling modes (grid, brick, half-drop, mirror and so on), and export the sheet at print DPI.
