My second printer, built January to March 2024. A Voron is not a kit: you get a bill of materials, a manual and STL files, and you source, print and assemble everything yourself. I re-drew the enclosure panels for a laser cutter (3 mm clear sides and top, 4 mm black back and floor) and printed the parts in red and black.

Since November 2025 a Bambu Lab A1 mini sits next to it for quick prints. In the lab I look after the Bambu P1SC, A1 and H2D printers and print most of our experiment fixtures: reflector mounts, a shaker connector, a ball bracket, quick-release plates and a radar shell.
