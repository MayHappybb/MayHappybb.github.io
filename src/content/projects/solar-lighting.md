An entry for the national energy-saving competition, February to July 2020, mostly built at home during the COVID semester. Fresnel lenses concentrate sunlight into optical fibres that carry it indoors. A two-axis gimbal with two steppers and a home-made light sensor keeps the lenses on the sun. Indoors, BH1750 lux sensors drive a boost constant-current LED board with PWM, holding the room at a set brightness when the sun fades. A solar panel and a battery power it all.

I did all the circuit design, PCB soldering, the control algorithm and the programming. The work became a utility model (2021) and an invention patent (2025), with me as third of six inventors.
